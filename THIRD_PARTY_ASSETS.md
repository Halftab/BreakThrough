## Hoverboard artwork

The original Flaticon hoverboard artwork used during development has been **removed from the F-Droid preparation build**. It is not redistributed by this build.

The replacement `www/assets/hoverboard.png` and its editable source `www/assets/hoverboard.svg` were created specifically for Break Through as original project artwork. They are released under the Apache License 2.0 with the rest of the project-created assets.

## Original Break Through assets

The Guy character, ground artwork, music, and the replacement hoverboard artwork were created specifically for the Break Through project and are released under the Apache License 2.0.
